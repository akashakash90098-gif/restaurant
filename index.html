CULINARY PHILOSOPHY

The Art of the Ember

We honor ancestral cooking techniques using seasoned white oak logs, 45-day dry-aged cuts, and wild-harvested botanicals sourced from certified sustainable producers.

01

Live Woodfire at 450°C

Every signature cut and blistered pizza meets live embers of seasoned white oak and hickory, imparting an unmistakable fragrant warmth and caramelization.

02

Farm-to-Hearth Provenance

HERITAGE & HEARTH

Rooted in Fire, Elevated by Craft

Ember & Oak was born from a singular passion: stripping away culinary pretense to celebrate the transformative magic of raw fire, wild smoke, and pristine earth.

Executive Chef Marcus Vance trained across San Sebastián, Lyon, and Tokyo before founding our hearth. In an era of sterile modern kitchens, we built our dining sanctuary around a 2,000-pound custom refractory hearth stoked with white oak and applewood charcoal.

“Smoke is not merely a flavor in our kitchen — it is the fifth element, an olfactory bridge between ancient ritual and modern elegance.”
Marcus Vance
Executive Chef & Co-Founder
